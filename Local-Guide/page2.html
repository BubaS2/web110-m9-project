Things to do

Parks, museums, and local attractions worth exploring.

Raleigh is full of easy outings for families and weekend visitors. Whether you want hands-on learning, outdoor time, or a relaxed city stroll, the Triangle offers a mix of fun that is simple to plan and easy to enjoy.

NC Museum of Natural Sciences

Explore fascinating exhibits, dinosaurs, and interactive displays that keep kids and adults engaged for hours.

NCMA Museum Park

Enjoy outdoor sculptures, open lawns, and a calm setting for a walk, picnic, or a break between city activities.

Pullen Park

A classic Raleigh destination with playgrounds, vintage rides, scenic paths, and lots of family-friendly fun.

Downtown Raleigh

Add an evening stroll, live music, or casual dining to round out a simple and exciting trip.

Best picks

Easy experiences for a weekend visit.

  • Start at a museum for interactive learning and discovery.
  • Spend a few hours walking through a park or outdoor art space.
  • Visit downtown after lunch for relaxed shopping and live music.
  • Plan one standout neighborhood stop for coffee or dessert.

Why visitors love it

Raleigh balances educational stops, outdoor fun, and laid-back city energy, making it perfect for effortless family outings.
