Rainy Day Adventure

Go curious, then take a cozy break.

NC Museum of Natural Sciences

Make this your main indoor stop for hands-on exhibits and dinosaur discoveries.

Tip: Check the exhibit schedule before you go and leave time for a favorite gallery.

Raleigh Times

Head downtown for a relaxed lunch after exploring the museum.

Tip: Check current hours and the menu before setting out.

Family Day on a Budget

Mix free outdoor fun with a little planning.

NCMA Museum Park

Explore outdoor art and open lawns at your own pace.

Tip: Pack a picnic and bring a blanket for a low-cost lunch break.

Pullen Park

Make time for the playground and an easy walk around the park.

Tip: Check ride schedules and prices ahead of time; the playground is a fun no-ride option.

NC Museum of Natural Sciences

Add a free museum visit for an indoor change of pace.

Tip: General admission is free; check ahead for hours and any special exhibit fees.

Why families love it

Easy adventures for all ages.

Parks and play

Families can enjoy relaxed outdoor fun, stroller-friendly paths, and plenty of open space for a low-stress afternoon.

Museums and learning

Interactive exhibits, hands-on discovery, and art-filled stops make Raleigh a great place to explore together.

Good food nearby

After a day outdoors, you can grab comfort food, coffee, or a casual dinner in a city that loves great local flavor.

Popular spots

Easy stops for a memorable trip.

  • NC Museum of Natural Sciences for interactive exhibits and curious minds
  • NCMA Museum Park for outdoor art and a relaxed scenic walk
  • Pullen Park for family-friendly rides, playgrounds, and lakeside paths
  • Downtown Raleigh for live music, casual dining, and evening fun

Plan a day

Simple ideas for first-time visitors.

  1. Start with a museum visit or a relaxed stroll through a park.
  2. Take a midday break for lunch or a coffee stop nearby.
  3. Spend the afternoon exploring downtown or a local neighborhood.

Quick tips

  • Best for families: parks and hands-on museums
  • Best for weekends: easy downtown outings
  • Good for: low-stress, memorable day trips
